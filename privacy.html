Privacy Policy

How I collect, use and look after your personal information.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Coaching only works when you can speak openly, so I take what you share with me seriously. This policy explains, in plain English, what personal information I collect, why I need it, who else handles it and what your rights are. If anything isn't clear, email me at hey@shaun.coach.

shaun.coach is run by Shaun Doherty, a sole trader based in Northern Ireland, UK. For the purposes of UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), I am the controller of your personal information. This means I decide how it's used and I'm responsible for protecting it.

2. What I collect

Information you give me

  • Contact details: your name, email address and, if you share it, your phone number. These come from enquiries, discovery-call bookings, free resource sign-ups and purchases.
  • Purchase details: what you bought, how much you paid, your billing address and your instalment status. I never see or store your full card details. Stripe and ThriveCart process payments securely.
  • Coaching information: the content of our sessions, my session notes and summaries, messages between sessions, and anything else you choose to share about your work, life, relationships, wellbeing or goals.
  • Session recordings: recordings or transcripts of our calls, made only if you agree to them (see section 4).
  • Feedback and testimonials: what you tell me about your experience. I only publish testimonials with your permission.

Information collected automatically

  • Email engagement: whether my emails are opened and which links are clicked, so that I can send content that's useful.

4. Sensitive information and session recordings

During coaching, you may tell me about your physical or mental health, or other personal matters that the law treats as special category data. I only use this information to coach you, and I process it on the basis of your explicit consent, which I ask for at the start of our work together. You decide what you share. You can withdraw your consent at any time, but I may then be unable to continue coaching you.

I only record sessions with your agreement. I use recordings and transcripts to prepare your session summaries and to review and develop my own coaching practice. If I use a transcription or AI tool to help with this, I choose providers that don't use your data to train their models. I never share recordings and never use them in marketing. Only anonymised, general insights would ever appear in my content, and never in a way that could identify you.

Coaching is not therapy or medical treatment. If you share something that suggests you or someone else is at serious risk of harm, I may encourage you to seek appropriate professional support.

5. Who I share it with

I don't sell your personal information. I use a small number of trusted service providers, known as processors, to run my business. They can only use your information to provide their service to me:

  • Stripe and ThriveCart (payments and checkout)
  • Google (Google Meet video calls and recordings, Gmail and Google Drive)
  • Discord (messaging between sessions)
  • TidyCal (booking calls)
  • Kit (newsletters and free resources)
  • ManyChat (Instagram messaging)
  • Gamma (preparing session summaries)

I may also share information with my accountant, or where the law requires it.

Discord is a platform you'll also have your own account with. Its own privacy policy covers how Discord handles your account.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States or other countries outside the UK. When information leaves the UK, I make sure it is protected by an approved safeguard. Examples include the UK–US Data Bridge, a UK adequacy decision, or the standard contractual clauses approved by the UK Information Commissioner.

8. How I keep it safe

I protect your information with password-protected, two-factor-authenticated accounts and encrypted devices. I store as little as possible and only use reputable providers. No system is completely secure. If a breach affected your information and was likely to put you at risk, I would tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

9. Cookies

This website doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Videos on the site are embedded in privacy-enhanced modes (youtube-nocookie and Vimeo's "do not track") so that they don't track you. The checkout is hosted by ThriveCart and the booking page by TidyCal, and each has its own cookie policy. If I ever add cookies that need your consent, I'll update this policy and ask for your consent first.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information I hold about you
  • Correct anything that's inaccurate or incomplete
  • Have your information deleted, unless I need to keep it, for example for tax records
  • Restrict or object to how I use it, including for direct marketing
  • Receive your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where consent is my legal basis

To use any of these rights, email hey@shaun.coach. I'll reply within one month. It's free in almost all cases.

I don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

11. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first so that I can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

12. Changes to this policy

I'll update this policy when the way I work changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. If I make a significant change that affects current clients, I'll let you know directly.
