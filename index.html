The secret success trap keeping you stuck

You're driven, you're ambitious. You've put the hours in, earned the salary, enjoyed the perks. So why do you feel a little... lost? The Success Trap is where you were told happiness is hitting 50k, then 100k, then 500k. Getting top biller. You were told this would feel incredible; however, it can bring more stress than it solves. You hit your targets, climb the ladder, build the business and NOTHING.

You then set a BIGGER target, believing this one will be different, but doing more of the same gets you more of the same.

  • You've set yourself the targets, worked yourself to the bone, hit your goal and felt nothing (so why keep trying?)
  • No matter what you earn there's a feeling of 'this isn't enough' which allows you to be top performer in business, but it can (and will) have a huge impact on your personal life
  • You've built the business, but can't enjoy it because if you switch off, even for a week, the whole thing will fall apart.
  • What was a goal in the early part of your career is now impacting your peace of mind which in turn is making you less effective at work.
  • You constantly beat yourself up for how you feel, because to anyone looking in from the outside, you 'have it all'

The Principles behind the Peace, Power & Performance System point to one truth lost in the drive for more. Your Clarity, your presence, your focus, calm and happiness are never outside you.

The Peace, Power & Performance framework

Everything changes from the inside out.

01

Peace

Peace is two-fold. One, it's the driver of all your actions. Everything you do as a human, at some level, is built on the belief you'll experience more peace for doing it. Two, Peace is your natural state. Not something you have to earn, and not something which needs to be outsourced to external goals.

02

Power

Power is when you understand the true nature of your experience. No event has the power to make you feel anything; it is your thinking about the event which causes your feelings about it. This puts your power with you. Not with external happenings.

03

Performance

When your Peace and Power are in alignment, the Performance takes care of itself. The difference being, instead of you having to push towards goals, targets and growth, you are pulled there. In short, you use a much more efficient and sustainable fuel source for your goals.

What you might be wondering

What is Principles-Based coaching, how is it different?

Most coaching works on behaviour, habits, or mindset, what you do and how you think. The Peace, Power & Performance System works upstream of that. It points to the fundamental nature of how your thought creates experience. When you begin to understand this, change happens naturally and without the usual effort because you're addressing the source, not the symptoms.

Do I need to be in crisis or struggling to benefit from this?

No. Many clients are objectively successful and functioning well. But there's usually a recognition that something is missing, or that they're operating from a place of pressure rather than Peace. If this sounds familiar, the work is relevant regardless of how things look from the outside.

How are sessions delivered?

Sessions are delivered on Google Meet, typically bi-weekly, across six months. They're conversational in nature, not structured presentations or worksheets. The work comes from the conversations and what you bring to each call.

What if I'm not sure this is right for me yet?

That's a fair place to be. If you want to have a conversation before committing, you can reach out directly at hey@shaun.coach. The 60-day guarantee also means you can begin with confidence knowing there's no risk if the work doesn't land for you.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching isn't therapy or medical treatment, and I don't diagnose or treat anything. Many clients find the work deeply therapeutic, but it's about understanding how your experience is created, not analysing your past. If you're currently in crisis or need clinical support, I'll always encourage you to work with a suitable professional.

How much time do I need between sessions?

Sessions are spaced at least two weeks apart. That gives you room to integrate what we've covered. There's no homework or worksheets. Most of the work happens as you go about your life and start noticing things differently.

What if I miss or need to move a session?

You pay for 12 sessions, and you get 12 sessions. If life gets in the way, we reschedule.

What happens after 6 months?

It's your decision. Some clients renew, some move to one call a month and some graduate completely. We'll talk about it towards the end of our 12 sessions.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, most do. There are six instalments of £300 or a one-off payment of £1,800.
